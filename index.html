CallWhat it does

      
createFace(el, opts)Mounts a face in el. Options: expression, mouth, pupils, follow, blink, wander, skin, ink, ring, gloss, rim, glow, scan, hue.

      
face.set(name)Morph to one of the 30 expressions. Unknown names throw an error that lists the real ones.

      
face.react(name)One-shot: blink wink nod shake bounce pop boing spin jitter.

      
face.talk(0..1)Speech energy, call it every frame. The mouth opens and closes with it.

      
face.update(opts)Change any option live.

      
face.destroy()Unmount and stop animating.

    

    

      
Cheap at any numberEvery face on the page shares one animation loop and one pointer listener, and faces scrolled out of view skip their frame. The wall above costs about what a handful of faces do.

      
Respects the userprefers-reduced-motion turns off wandering, blinking and the movement reactions, with no guard of your own.
